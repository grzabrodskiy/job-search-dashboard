## Notes on Sponsorship
- Large multinational banks and tech companies routinely sponsor work permits in Switzerland
- Switzerland uses a quota system for non-EU/EFTA workers; US/UK citizens fall under this quota
- Employers must demonstrate no suitable local candidate was found
- A senior title and specialized, in-demand skills make sponsorship more likely
- Companies marked "?" should be researched before applying — check if they mention visa sponsorship in job listings
